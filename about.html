About us

Two ideas have guided us from day one: build seating that holds up, and treat every customer like the project matters. Everything else follows from that.

A legacy company, not a start-up

Under the Raynor Group, Eurotech has designed, manufactured and distributed office seating for more than four decades. Today it's one of the most established names in the category, with a full range of office and school chairs engineered for the way people work now.

Having reached cumulative sales of over 1 billion dollars, Eurotech offers dependability you can expect from a legacy company.

By providing personalized, built-to-order service, Eurotech’s customers can easily spec their seating projects and get precisely what they’ve envisioned for the office or classroom.
