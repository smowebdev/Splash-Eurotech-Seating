Overview

Apollo covers the whole office in a single, coherent design — a mid-back task chair for focused work, a high-back with an adjustable headrest, and a full executive seat. Because every model shares the same frame, mechanism and materials, a room full of Apollos looks intentional, not assembled from spare parts.
