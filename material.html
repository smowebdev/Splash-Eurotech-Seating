Advanced Material Technologies

Outlast® Technology

Available on selected Fabrix chairs, Outlast Technology helps regulate temperature throughout the day by absorbing, storing, and releasing body heat for consistent seating comfort.

  • Controls temperature for a consistently cool, comfortable seat
  • Reduces moisture for all-day comfort
  • Proactively reduces perspiration — not just wicking it away
  • Absorbs, stores and releases body heat for a steady temperature
