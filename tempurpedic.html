What Makes TEMPUR® Different

Built to adapt, not just cushion.

TEMPUR® Material combines viscous and elastic properties, allowing it to react differently to every user.

Rather than simply compressing, the material continuously adjusts to movement and distributes pressure where it's needed most.

Pressure Relief

Reduces pressure on key contact points during extended sitting.

Adaptive Support

Responds naturally to body movement throughout the workday.

Premium Material

Long-Term Comfort

Designed for professionals who spend hours at their desk.

Responds to your body

TEMPUR® reacts to weight, shape and temperature, creating a seating experience that changes with you throughout the day.

Comfort without compromise

Unlike traditional foam, TEMPUR® maintains support while reducing concentrated pressure on key contact points.
